# CodeCrafters_Community
A space for passionate developers to craft modern web applications through teamwork and open-source contributions. Join fellow coders to develop skills, solve challenges, and create impactful digital solutions.

# For a developer community website, Restrictions & Agreement can be simple and professional.
# Restrictions
* Members must maintain respectful and professional behavior.
* No hate speech, harassment, bullying, or discrimination.
* No spam, advertising, or unauthorized promotions.
* Do not upload malicious code, viruses, or harmful content.
* Do not share copyrighted content without permission.
* Do not misuse community resources or access other users' data.
* Any content violating laws or community guidelines may be removed.
* Administrators reserve the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate these rules.
# Agreement
By joining this community, you agree to:
* Follow all community rules and guidelines.
* Respect other members and their contributions.
* Use the platform for learning, collaboration, and development purposes.
* Take responsibility for the content you post.
* Contribute in a positive and constructive manner.
* Accept that community administrators may moderate content to maintain a healthy environment.
* Understand that violations of these terms may result in account suspension or removal.
